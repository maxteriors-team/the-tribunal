What happens after your install

  1. Tonight: the system runs on the schedule we set — you don't have to do anything.
  2. This week: live with it. Note anything you'd aim differently once the plants settle and you've seen it a few nights.
  3. Anytime: text us the changes. Brightness, shutoff time, added fixtures, re-aiming — that's all normal service, not a complaint.

The short version of care

  • Keep mulch, leaves, and new growth off the lenses.
  • Warn whoever mows and edges that there's low-voltage wire in the beds.
  • After a season of growth, expect a few fixtures to want re-aiming.
  • Anything electrical, underground, or a fuse that blows twice — that's us, not you.
