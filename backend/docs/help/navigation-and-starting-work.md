# Navigation, Today, Dashboard, and Nudges

Use the left sidebar to move between the CRM's main screens. Press Command-K on macOS or Control-K on Windows to open **Search...**, then type a screen or action name. Some screens are hidden when the signed-in role does not have the required capability.

## Work the Today queue

Route: `/today`. Sidebar label: **Today**.

1. Open **Today**.
2. Work the numbered mission queue from top to bottom. Items can include **Reply needed**, **Appointments**, **Approvals**, **Nudges**, **Fresh batch**, **Draft campaign**, and **Setup**.
3. Select the button on an item to open the exact record or screen that needs attention.
4. Select **Start my day** to open the Assistant with a daily briefing at `/assistant?briefing=1`.

Today is an action queue, not a report. If it says **All clear**, there is no queued action right now; select **Ask the assistant anyway** for a briefing.

## Read the Dashboard

Route: `/dashboard`. Sidebar label: **Dashboard**.

The Dashboard summarizes Total Contacts, Active Campaigns, Calls Today, Messages Sent, monthly pace, revenue and lead-source ROI, appointment performance, speed to lead, reviews, roleplay, knowledge-base health, recent activity, campaigns, agents, and today's overview. Select a card to open its source screen when the card is linked. Use **New Campaign** or **View Contacts** for the two primary shortcuts.

## Handle Nudges

Route: `/nudges`. Sidebar label: **Nudges**.

1. Open **Nudges** and choose a status tab such as **Pending**.
2. Review the reminder and its related contact.
3. Complete its action, **Snooze** it until a chosen date, or **Dismiss** it.
4. Use the Pending, Sent, Acted, and Total cards to understand the current queue.

Nudges are relationship reminders and follow-up prompts. Configure their behavior under **Settings → Nudges** at `/settings?tab=nudges`.

## Use the Assistant

Route: `/assistant`. Sidebar label: **Assistant**.

Ask for CRM facts, safe changes, outreach workflows, daily priorities, or step-by-step product help. The Assistant can show previews and approval cards for consequential outbound actions. It cannot expose data from another workspace or bypass the signed-in user's role.
