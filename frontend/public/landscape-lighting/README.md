# Landscape lighting photos

Drop real photos of Maxteriors installs here, then register each one in
`src/components/landscape-landing/project-gallery.tsx`.

Guidance:

- **Real work only.** No stock, no AI-generated houses, no competitor photos.
  A homeowner compares this page to what shows up on the design visit.
- **Shoot after dark**, which is the only time the product exists. A daytime
  photo of a fixture sells nothing.
- **Get permission** before publishing a customer's home, and avoid house
  numbers and plates in frame.
- Export web-sized (roughly 1600px wide, JPEG or WebP) so the page stays fast.
